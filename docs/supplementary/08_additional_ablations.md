# Additional Ablations

This appendix summarizes supplementary ablation families represented by the
repository. These assets provide additional detail and are not numbered as current paper figures.

## Context Length

Context-length behavior is represented by supplementary figure and evidence
files:

- `figures/supplementary/modernbert_context_ablation.pdf`
- `figures/supplementary/modernbert_context_ablation.png`
- `evidence/locked_sources/context_length/modernbert_context_ablation.csv`
- `evidence/locked_sources/context_length/llm_context_length_meanstd.csv`

## Domain-Specific Pretraining

Domain-adapted and pretrained-model comparisons are represented by supplementary
figure and evidence files:

- `figures/supplementary/llm_pretraining_ablation.pdf`
- `figures/supplementary/llm_pretraining_ablation.png`
- `evidence/locked_sources/llm_comparison/llm_pretraining_meanstd.csv`

## Encoder-Decoder Comparison

Encoder and decoder comparisons are represented by supplementary figure and
evidence files:

- `figures/supplementary/encoder_decoder_comparison.pdf`
- `figures/supplementary/encoder_decoder_comparison.png`
- `evidence/locked_sources/llm_comparison/encoder_decoder_comparison.csv`

## Robustness and Sensitivity

Compact robustness and sensitivity summaries are packaged in
`evidence/robustness_sensitivity/robustness_sensitivity_summary.csv`. These summaries consolidate the available repeated-seed and window-size evidence.
